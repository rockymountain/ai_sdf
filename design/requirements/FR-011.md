# Intent

A governed DEV and measurement window may deliberately use different runtime and
model treatments for different invocation purposes while retaining one SDF
execution, safety, attribution, and acceptance boundary.

# Acceptance

Every permitted purpose-specific treatment is explicit and includes runtime adapter,
requested model, capability-aware requested reasoning effort, model-selection
strategy, optional routing-policy version, and context strategy. Window-level
automatic-routing and context-optimization flags remain explicit. No treatment is
inherited from another purpose and no runtime is selected automatically.

The existing `invocation_purpose` values are the execution roles. No parallel agent,
builder, or auditor role field is introduced. Implementation and valid continuation
may receive workspace mutation only through their existing governed attempt lineage.
`review`, `acceptance_validation`, and `orchestration` remain read-only regardless of
runtime adapter selection.

Every controlled invocation, including implementation, continuation, review,
acceptance-validation, and orchestration where used, remains attributable to its
owning DEV. Reports retain DEV totals and deterministic breakdowns by invocation
purpose and runtime adapter. Failed, rejected, retried, and review usage remains
visible.

For every retained in-window invocation, treatment consistency is evaluated against
the declaration for that invocation's purpose. An undeclared purpose, missing
runtime identity, or treatment mismatch remains visible, retains its usage, and
prevents exact treatment-dependent KPI eligibility. Missing usage remains unknown,
never zero.

Provider/model identifiers are declaration data rather than factory invariants.
The intended future control treatment may explicitly choose Claude for implementation
and continuation and Codex for review, but the generic measurement engine must not
require that combination.
