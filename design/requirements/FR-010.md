# Intent

QG-006 intentionally keeps semantic-risk and level-classification judgment human.
The repository currently states that obligation but does not define authoritative,
machine-verifiable evidence that the required reviewer acted on the exact governed
change.

# Requirement

Where QG-006 requires accountable human review, merge or transfer eligibility must
have verifiable evidence that an authorized reviewer made an explicit disposition
for the exact governed revision or change.

Machine validation may verify evidence presence, source authority, reviewer
identity and authorization, revision binding, disposition, freshness, and replay
resistance. It must never infer, reproduce, or substitute the human semantic-risk
or level-classification judgment.

Missing, stale, unauthorized, ambiguous, rejected, or contract-forbidden self-
approval evidence must not satisfy QG-006.

# Acceptance boundary

- An accepted authority contract identifies the trusted review-evidence source.
- Review evidence binds to the exact governed revision or change.
- Approval and rejection are explicit; rejection blocks eligibility.
- Deterministic PASS is never represented as human approval.
- Runtime `HumanAuthorization` is not silently substituted for PR or design review
  authority.
- The evidence contract remains auditable and provider/tool neutral where the
  selected source permits.

# Lifecycle boundary

This requirement is proposed for Project Owner review. It does not select the
review-evidence source or authorize QG-006 implementation.
