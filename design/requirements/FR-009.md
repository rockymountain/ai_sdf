# Intent

FR-003 already requires per-task traceability and risk attestations for significant
PR changes. The remaining gap is that QG-003 traceability-policy and QG-005 risk-
attestation still combine canonical data with permissive defaults and duplicated
hard-coded interpretation.

# Requirement

Traceability-level requirements, escalation settings, and risk-attestation
applicability must come from complete, validated canonical policy. Required fields
must fail closed when absent or malformed rather than disappearing through
permissive defaults.

Risk-attestation applicability must follow the canonical level policy, and its
required content and shape must have an authoritative governed contract. A proposed
change must not waive the controls required to validate that same change.

# Acceptance boundary

- QG-003 and QG-005 declarations are validated and bound to their applicable
  canonical policy.
- Trace-level requirements and escalation settings are complete and fail closed.
- `requires.risk_attestation` determines applicability without a duplicate T1/T2
  condition in enforcement code.
- Base and proposed governance are evaluated so proposed weakening cannot
  self-authorize.
- Human semantic-risk and level-classification judgment remains human-owned.
- Existing FR-003, change-provenance, implementation-path, and QG-004 semantics
  remain intact.
