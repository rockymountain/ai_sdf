# Context

CMP-002 reads parts of the traceability-level policy, but missing nested fields can
currently become false through permissive defaults. Risk-attestation applicability
and required phrases are also interpreted by hard-coded T1/T2 logic rather than by
an authoritative QG-005 contract. QG-003 and QG-005 therefore share one policy-
validation and self-protection boundary while retaining separate gate identities.

# Proposed decision

1. Validate the complete supported traceability-level requirement shape and the
   supported escalation settings before using them.
2. Treat missing, malformed, unknown, duplicated, or unsupported mandatory policy
   as a closed gate, never as a false or absent requirement.
3. Validate and bind the QG-003 declaration to canonical traceability policy.
4. Validate and bind the QG-005 declaration to a governed risk-attestation contract.
5. Derive risk-attestation applicability from each level's canonical
   `requires.risk_attestation` value rather than a duplicated T1/T2 condition.
6. Evaluate applicable base and proposed governance independently so a proposed
   change cannot waive the controls that govern that change.
7. Reuse CMP-002, ADR-003 provenance behavior, and the ADR-004 self-protection
   pattern. Do not introduce a generic policy interpreter.
8. Preserve human accountability for semantic level classification and material
   risk judgment. Deterministic validation checks declarations and evidence; it
   does not decide semantic truth.

# Compatibility and exclusions

FR-003 remains historical and unchanged. Existing T0/T1/T2 evidence, implementation-
path resolution, change provenance, and QG-004 behavior must remain compatible.
QG-006 evidence and the QG-004 compatibility adapter are outside this decision.

# Owner decision

This ADR is proposed. Project Owner acceptance is required before DEV-015 may become
active or the canonical policy contract may change.
